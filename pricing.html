Common questions

Do you offer refunds? +
Yes, we offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans if you're not satisfied for any reason. After 14 days, monthly subscriptions can be canceled anytime with no penalty.
Can I switch plans anytime? +
Absolutely. Upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. If you upgrade, we'll prorate the difference. If you downgrade, credits will be applied to your next billing cycle.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), as well as PayPal and bank transfers for annual plans. All payments are processed securely through Stripe.
Do you offer team or enterprise plans? +
Yes! For teams needing custom limits, SSO, or dedicated support, reach out to our sales team at sales@clipwise.ai. We'll build a plan that fits your needs.
Do you provide API access? +
API access is available on the Studio plan. You'll get webhooks, batch processing endpoints, and custom model training. Full documentation is in your dashboard.
